# Promo Card Art Direction

All cards: 16:9 aspect ratio, 1920x1080 PNG or JPG.

Brand palette to match:
- Code Black background: #0E0E12
- Plasma Orange accent: #FF5E00
- White text: #FFFFFF

Style: Premium, dark, dramatic. Think GTA Online loading screens, Stake.com promo banners,
Apex Legends update slides. Heavy contrast, focal element with rim light or particle effects.

## Card 1 — winner-spotlight.png

Concept: A dramatic chess piece (king or queen, glossy black) with orange light radiating
behind it. Big text overlay area for "shadowfox WON +500 SP" — leave the right 60% darker
so text reads cleanly. Particle/spark effects.

## Card 2 — founders-spots.png

Concept: Glowing hexagonal Founders badge (use the actual /images/badge-founders.png look)
floating center, dark embery background, urgency vibe. Text area: "FOUNDERS PROGRAM —
47 SPOTS REMAIN". Premium, scarce, exclusive feel.

## Card 3 — tournament-hype.png

Concept: A trophy or bracket/tournament structure, dramatic spotlight from above, dark
arena background. Text area: "SATURDAY SHOWDOWN — 5,000 SP PRIZE POOL". Big stakes feel.

## Card 4 — rank-diamond.png

Concept: The Diamond rank shield (matches /images/rank-diamond.png aesthetic) shattering
through a dark surface, blue/cyan glow, particle effects. Text area: "phoenix77 — DIAMOND
TIER REACHED".

## Card 5 — new-game-drop.png

Concept: Bold "NEW GAME" or "NOW LIVE" treatment with one of the game card images
(e.g. trivia-card.png) angled and glowing. Reveal-poster aesthetic. Text area: "TRIVIA —
SPEED KNOWLEDGE DUEL".

## Card 6 — win-streak.png

Concept: Fire/flame motif, glowing meter or counter showing "8 WINS", intense red-orange
gradient. Text area: "kira_06 — 8-WIN STREAK". Hot-streak energy.

## Generation tips

- Generate at 1920x1080 (16:9 desktop hero size)
- Leave at least 30-40% of the card with a darker zone where overlay text would sit
- Avoid text in the AI-generated image itself (you can add text in Photoshop/Canva after,
  or skip text and let the visual carry it)
- Keep the orange (#FF5E00) as the dominant accent in 4 of 6 cards
- Use blue/cyan only for Diamond rank card (Card 4)
- Match the existing game card art style (saturated, 3D, dramatic lighting)
